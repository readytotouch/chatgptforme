Use {{.Engine.Short}} from your address bar

Add a custom search engine to your browser with the URL {{.Site.URL}}/{{.Engine.Slug}}/?go=1&q=%s and a shortcut such as {{.Engine.Slug}}. Typing {{.Engine.Slug}} how to boil rice then sends the question straight to {{.Engine.Short}}. In Chrome this lives under Settings, Search engine, Manage search engines and site search.
