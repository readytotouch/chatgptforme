How the Gemini link works

Gemini has no URL parameter for a pre-filled prompt. Copy the prompt and paste it into the chat.

Other assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity accept a prompt in the URL. Until Google adds the same, this page keeps your prompt one click away from the clipboard.
