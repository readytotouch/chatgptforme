How the Google AI Mode link works

Google AI Mode by Google reads the prompt from a URL parameter. The link this page builds has the form https://www.google.com/search?udm=50&q=YOUR+PROMPT, with the prompt URL-encoded.

Google Search with the AI Mode answer (udm=50) instead of the classic results list.

No account needed, availability depends on your country.

Use Google AI Mode from your address bar

Add a custom search engine to your browser with the URL https://searchgptforme.com/google-ai-mode/?go=1&q=%s and a shortcut such as google-ai-mode. Typing google-ai-mode how to boil rice then sends the question straight to Google AI Mode. In Chrome this lives under Settings, Search engine, Manage search engines and site search.

Share a Google AI Mode prompt

Instead of telling someone "just ask Google AI Mode", send them the link: it opens Google AI Mode with your exact prompt. A link to this page, for example https://searchgptforme.com/google-ai-mode/?q=explain+quantum+computing, lets them review the prompt first.
